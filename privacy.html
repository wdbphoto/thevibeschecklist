Privacy

The Vibes Checklist · last updated November 18, 2026

Everything stays on your device

Your choices, checklists, check-ins, reflections and birthday are saved only in your own browser's storage. There are no accounts and no database of users, and we never see your journal.

What leaves your device

  • Fonts load from Google Fonts and Adobe Fonts, which see a normal web request from your browser.
  • Camera (Vibe Mirror) stays on your device. Photos are never uploaded; saving or sharing is always your choice.
  • Shared links carry your vibe code (your choices, never your journal or birthday) to whoever you send them to.

Advertising

The site may show a small number of ads from Google AdSense to keep it free. Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads, and visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and turn off personalized ads in Google's ad settings.

What we do not do

No analytics of our own, no tracking pixels, no accounts, and nothing sold to anyone.

A gentle note

The Vibes Checklist is for reflection and fun. Astrology, crystal lore and feng shui are traditions, not science, and nothing here is medical or mental-health advice. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional.
