Privacy

The Vibes Checklist · last updated November 17, 2026

Everything stays on your device

Your choices, checklists, check-ins, reflections and birthday are saved only in your own browser's storage. There are no accounts and no database of users, and we never see your journal.

What leaves your device

  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees a normal web request from your browser.
  • Shared links carry your vibe code (your choices, never your journal or birthday) to whoever you send them to.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking pixels, no analytics cookies, and nothing sold to anyone.

A gentle note

The Vibes Checklist is for reflection and fun. Astrology, crystal lore and feng shui are traditions, not science, and nothing here is medical or mental-health advice. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional.
